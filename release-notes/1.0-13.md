## Seen & Unseen Update

This update reworks how Ava handles seen and hidden posts, fixes various media playback issues, and makes new comments easier to spot. It also brings more customization options, improved on-device summaries, and a little Vice to the app icon collection.

### What's Changed

#### Fixes

* 'View NSFW Content' button can have black text color if the post is marked as a Spoiler as well
* An issue where tapping a trophy in profile could resize trophies to 0 for a split second
* Reddit Endpoints under-the-hood fixes
* An issue where GIFs are playing outside playback trigger area
* NSFW/Spoiler and RedGIFs content prevents autoplay
* NSFW/Spoiler marked cross-posts with child post not marked as NSFW/Spoiler autoplays under blur
* An issue where disabling looping videos prevents restarting video playback manually
* An issue where video audio could keep playing after leaving fullscreen and scrolling away, sometimes overlapping 
  with other videos.

#### New Features

* New comments highlighter toggle in settings
* Thumbnail position picker for Compact Posts (left, right, disabled)
* Added new app icon - **Ava Vice** for fellow gamers
  * **Disclaimer:** Any resemblance to existing logos, artwork, or intellectual property, living or dead, is purely 
    coincidental.

#### Improvements

* Increased prominence of new comments badge
* Increased prominence of highlighted comments
* Reworked seen/hide system from scratch
* Increased limit of fetched posts when Compact Posts is enabled (to reduce API calls)
* Tweaked on-device model based on summaries reports
* Increased tappable area of **Skip Comment** button for better reachability
* Other smaller improvements
